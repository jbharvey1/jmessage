# JMessage Demo Voiceover

JMessage starts with a theme picker. Browse through seven built-in themes. Each one previews live as you scroll. Pick one and you're in.

Hit Control-O to see your settings. Theme, notification bell, message history limit, and your relay connection, all in one place.

Press Escape to see your conversations. Names are resolved from your Mac's contacts automatically. Tab filters to known contacts only. Arrow keys to navigate, Enter to open.

Messages appear in real time. Type a reply and hit Enter. It sends instantly. Blue names for iMessage, green for SMS, just like the real thing. Notifications from other people pop in as they arrive.

Control-A opens the attachment browser. Every file, image, and URL from the conversation. Download individually, grab all images as a zip, or extract every link to a text file.

Control-T cycles through all seven themes instantly. Your messages re-render in the new colors on the fly. The world's first terminal-native iMessage client. Built for developers who live in the keyboard.
